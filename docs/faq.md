# LSNC FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the **LibreShare Non-Commercial License (LSNC)**. See the [Versions](version.md) page for available versions.

## What is the LSNC?

The LSNC is an open-source license that allows non-commercial use, modification, and sharing of software, with a copyleft requirement to share source code. It prohibits commercial use and reverse engineering of compiled versions.

## Can I use the LSNC for my project?

Yes! The LSNC is designed to be used by anyone. Simply include the license file or a link to it in your project and comply with its terms.

## Can I use LSNC software in a school or university?

Yes, the LSNC explicitly allows non-commercial educational use, such as in schools, universities, or training programs, as long as it remains non-commercial.

## Can I use LSNC software for commercial purposes?

No, commercial use is prohibited unless you obtain written permission from the Rights Holder ([lameur.tech@gmail.com](mailto:lameur.tech@gmail.com)).

## What is copyleft?

Copyleft means that any derivative work based on LSNC-licensed software must be distributed under the same license and include the source code.

## Can I combine LSNC software with software under another license?

Combining LSNC software with software under a different license requires prior agreement from the Rights Holder. Contact [lameur.tech@gmail.com](mailto:lameur.tech@gmail.com) for details.

## How do I contribute to the LSNC?

We welcome suggestions to improve the LSNC! Open an [issue](https://github.com/Lameur/LSNC-License/issues) or submit a [pull request](https://github.com/Lameur/LSNC-License/pulls) on [GitHub](https://github.com/Lameur).

## Where can I get help?

Contact the Rights Holder at [lameur.tech@gmail.com](mailto:lameur.tech@gmail.com) or via [github.com/Lameur](https://github.com/Lameur) for any questions or requests.
